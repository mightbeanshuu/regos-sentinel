## Narration

1. **Hook** — SEBI's cyber-security framework tells a broker to close a high
   severity finding within one week. It never says one week from when. Every
   firm has to answer that question, and answer it the same way twice.

2. **Source** — RegOS Sentinel starts at the source itself: the exact passage,
   the page it sits on, and a fingerprint of the document it was read from.
   Nothing here is summarised, and nothing is invented.

3. **Interpret** — It reads that passage against the firm's own control. The
   period is stated. The starting point is not. So no due date is calculated.
   The clock stays blocked, and the gap goes in front of a person.

4. **Decide** — The decision belongs to a named officer. RegOS will not accept
   it until every section the answer depends on has been read and fingerprinted.
   The reading is time-stamped before the draft interpretation is revealed.

5. **Impact** — With the clock-start recorded, the deadline computes, the checks
   pass, and one broad control splits into the two rules the source actually
   described.

6. **Proof** — The record downloads as a compliance build report: the decision,
   the person who made it, the evidence behind it, and the check codes that tie
   all three together.

7. **Your document** — Point it at your own circular. This is a live SEBI PDF —
   seven pages read, fifty-eight passages classified, and a deadline-clarity
   score computed from that file alone.

8. **Close** — RegOS Sentinel. Decision support that shows its work, and stops
   where the source stops.
